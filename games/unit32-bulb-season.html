Unit 32 Bulb Season

Run the bulb department for one season

You manage the bulb side of a garden center from fall planting to the spring flower sale. Every right call earns sales. Wrong calls cost you customers.

  1. Sorting Day: spring planting or fall planting?
  2. Bed Prep Crew: soil, lime, fertilizer, and spacing
  3. Customer Desk: landscape uses, care after flowering, mice, lilies
  4. Bulb Doctor: match the damage to the pest or disease
  5. Forcing House: get flowers ready for a sale date

Every answer shows the reason and the textbook page. Keep your book open.
