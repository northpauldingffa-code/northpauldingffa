Welcome, new curator

The county museum is opening a new wing called Better Living through Agriscience. You run five halls. Visitors will ask questions, and you will set up exhibits. Every correct answer brings in more visitors and earns stars for your museum.

  1. Hall of Words: key terms
  2. Inventors' Gallery: who invented what, plus a timeline exhibit
  3. Living Environments Hall: homes, water, climate, insects, and chemicals
  4. Data Lab: population pyramids and farm math
  5. Research Wing: breakthroughs that solved real problems
