Wildlife Management CDE Practice

Each team member competes in one practicum. Find your section below. Every practicum is 60 minutes and worth 100 points, and all four scores count toward the team total.

🦌 Whitetail Deer 100 pts

Up to 40 items: jawbone aging, Boone and Crockett antler measuring to the nearest 1/8 inch, the four standard management objectives, and questions from Deer Herd Management for Georgia Hunters.

Whitetail Deer Management Practice Test183 questions from 4 past exams and the deer booklet, plus unlimited jawbone and antler drills

Up to 50 items from 33 wildlife species, 49 wildlife plants, and 18 pieces of equipment. Items can be photos, mounts, tracks, fruits, cones, or written descriptions.
